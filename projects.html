Gael — Invest by strategy, across every broker

I built Gael because I invest through several Mexican and US brokers and got tired of not knowing my real allocation. You log each trade once, and it works out what to buy or sell to get back on target. It never trades for you. Cost basis and P&L aren't stored anywhere; they're recalculated from the transaction log every time, so a stock split never corrupts the history.
